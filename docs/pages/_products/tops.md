Tops covers closed/open/pedal hi-hats, crash, ride, splash, china, cowbell, tambourine and triangle with multiple synthesis engines. The Instrument parameter picks which one a note plays (or switch Note Map to General MIDI drum notes); a closed or pedal hat chokes an open hat.
