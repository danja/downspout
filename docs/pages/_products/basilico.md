## Functionality

Basilico is a generator-friendly bass synth intended to sit under `bassgen`,
`ground`, and other MIDI sources. It combines stable bass pitch, glide,
velocity accent, body controls, and drive models across five bass characters.
Its wobble section can run free or tempo-synced, with separate amplitude,
filter, and phase/flange targets, while `Squelch` pushes Acid-style filter
snap and resonance into any model.
