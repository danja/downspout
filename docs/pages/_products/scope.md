title: Scope
description: Spectrum analyser and level meter with 64 labelled bands.
---

Scope passes audio through untouched and shows a 64-band spectrum (20 Hz to 20 kHz,
log-spaced and labelled) or per-channel peak/RMS meters with correlation. Choose a
60, 90 or 120 dB range, set the ballistics with Speed, and switch peak hold on or off.
No latency and no plugin state.
