# Resonance Garden

Resonance Garden turns stereo audio into pitched material with up to twelve
bounded damped resonators (Voice limit 1-12). MIDI notes retune voices; without
MIDI an internal scale keeps the bank musically useful. The scale is chosen from
a dropdown of BassGen's 23 scales and the root from the Internal root slider: voice
1 sits on the root (C3 plus root) and each further voice on the next scale degree,
climbing through the octaves. The bank sustains on that scale while no note is held;
a held note takes over a voice, and a released voice returns to the scale once its
tail has died away.
